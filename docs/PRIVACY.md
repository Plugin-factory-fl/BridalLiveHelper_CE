# BridalLive Helper privacy policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026. Version 0.0.1.

Public URL for the Chrome Web Store: **https://bridallivehelper-ce.onrender.com/privacy**

BridalLive Helper is a Chrome side panel used beside [BridalLive](https://app.bridallive.com) by boutique staff. It is published for The Chic Boutique (White Plains and Poughkeepsie).

## What we collect

- Helper account email, display name, and a hashed password (stored on the Helper server).
- A sign-in token and the working boutique (White Plains or Poughkeepsie) in Chrome storage on that computer.
- Inventory, receiving, and label requests needed to do the job, sent through the Helper server to BridalLive.

## What we do not collect

- We do not sell personal information.
- We do not use the data for advertising.
- Staff do not paste BridalLive API keys into the extension. Those keys stay on the Helper server.

## Who can see it

Only the shop that runs this Helper, and BridalLive as needed to look up inventory and print labels. The developer who hosts the Helper server can access server logs and the account file to keep the product running.

## Retention

Helper accounts stay until an administrator removes them. Sign-in sessions expire after 30 days or when you sign out. Chrome storage on a computer is cleared if you remove the extension or sign out.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: ask your shop manager or the person who hosts BridalLive Helper.
