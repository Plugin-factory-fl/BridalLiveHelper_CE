Before you print

  1. Scale: 100% — do not use Fit to page or Shrink to fit.
  2. Paper: US Letter (8.5″ × 11″).
  3. Labels: Avery 5160 / 8160 or compatible 30-up sheet.
  4. Run a test print on plain paper held over a sheet to check alignment first.
